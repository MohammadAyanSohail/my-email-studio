Steel names can feel like a maze when you are new to knives. We would rather tell you what MagnaCut does, and what it does not.

**What it does well.** MagnaCut holds an edge through everyday camp jobs, like slicing food or cutting cord. It is also rust and corrosion resistant. That means it copes well with damp air and wet hands. Resistant is not the same as rust-proof, so we do not say proof.

**What it still needs.** No blade stays sharp forever. Wipe it dry after use. Keep it clean. Sharpen it when it starts to slip. A few minutes of care keeps a dependable knife dependable.

**What it is not for.** Very hard steel can snap if it is pried, hammered or twisted. The V7 is built for cutting. Use a different tool for the rest.
