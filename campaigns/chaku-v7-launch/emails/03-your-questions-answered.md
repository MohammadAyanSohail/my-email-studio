Some questions come up again and again. Here are plain answers.

**What is the V7 for?**
Cutting work at camp and on the trail. It is a utility knife, nothing more.

**What is the blade made of?**
CPM MagnaCut steel. It holds an edge and is rust and corrosion resistant.

**Does it need care?**
Yes. Dry it, clean it and sharpen it when it needs it. No blade stays sharp forever.

**Can I pry or hammer with it?**
No. Hard steel can snap under that kind of use. Keep it for cutting.

**Can you ship to me?**
We cannot promise that shipping is simple or legal everywhere. The package is declared as a weapon when it ships. Rules differ by country and sometimes by region. Please check your local regulations before you order.

**Is it a good gift?**
It can be, for someone who camps or hikes. Check the rules where they live first. The V7 page has the shipping facts.
