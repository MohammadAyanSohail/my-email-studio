## 1. Campaign overview

- **Name:** Out There: The Chaku V7 Launch
- **Summary:** A four-week email series that invites our 2,000 waitlist members to start a Chaku subscription with the V7.
- **Main goal:** 300 subscriptions from the waitlist by 3 January 2027. That is 15% of the list.
- **Second goals:**
  - Learn which waitlist group responds best.
  - Collect real questions and objections for later campaigns.
  - Keep unsubscribes and spam complaints low, so the list stays healthy.

### Is 300 realistic?

It is a stretch. In my judgment, 15% is high for an email-only launch, even to people who asked to hear from us. We have no past results to test this against, so treat the target as ambitious until week 1 shows real numbers.

Here is one example of what it would take:

| Step | People | Share |
|------|--------|-------|
| Waitlist | 2,000 | 100% |
| Open at least one email | 1,400 | 70% of list |
| Visit the sign-up page | 700 | 50% of openers |
| Start a subscription | 300 | 43% of visitors |

These are example numbers, not forecasts. The last step is the hardest, so a clear offer and a strong sign-up page matter most.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** The Chaku V7 is a dependable MagnaCut knife, built to last for real nights out there.

**Supporting messages**

| Message | Proof | Do we have it? |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| MagnaCut holds an edge, with regular care. | Steel named in the brand guide | Yes. Add V7 specs. |
| MagnaCut is rust and corrosion resistant. | Steel named in the brand guide | Yes. Always say "resistant". |
| Built for cutting work at camp and on the trail, not for abuse. | Clear guidance on intended use | Yes. Add a care guide. |
| Tested by real campers and hikers. | Named quotes or test notes | Not yet. Cut this if we can't prove it. |
| The subscription is worth starting. | Contents and price | Not yet. See question 1. |

**Message order in each email:** why this matters out there, what the V7 is, why Chaku, then one clear next step.

**How the tone shifts by group**

- **Campers and hikers:** direct and detailed. Talk like someone who has been there.
- **Beginners:** patient and clear. Explain terms without talking down.
- **Gift buyers:** warm and practical. Remove doubts and make the choice easy.

**Voice rules for every email**

- Sound like a fellow camper: plain, calm and warm.
- Say "brother" no more than once in an email, and only where it feels natural.
- Sign off with: the Chaku Smiths
- Keep the V7 a utility knife. No self-defense, protection, fighting, combat, tactical or weapon-style language.
- Never say a blade never dulls, can't break, or is rust-proof.
- Include a line in every email with a sign-up link: check your local regulations before ordering. For gifts, that means the rules where the recipient lives.
- Don't promise that shipping is simple or legal everywhere.
- No hype, all capitals or strings of exclamation marks.

## 9. Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | What we do about it |
|------|--------------------|
| 15% is too high for this list. | Check at the end of week 1. If we're under 90, focus on clickers, fix the page, and tell you early that the target may fall short. |
| The subscription isn't clear, or doesn't fit knife buyers. | Settle question 1 first, and test the page on a few people. |
| Some buyers can't legally receive the knife. | Filter by country if we can. Include the regulations line every time. Promise nothing about delivery. |
| An email reads like a self-defense pitch. | Check every draft against the brand guide. Show the V7 only as a camp and trail tool. |
| The campaign runs through Christmas and New Year. | No sends from 23 to 27 December. Keep the December sends early in the week. |
| Gift delivery misses Christmas. | State only confirmed timing. If it can't arrive, say so plainly. |
| An old list gets poor delivery. | Clean it first, confirm these people agreed to email, and set up domain checks. Include an unsubscribe link and your business address. |
| Too many emails tire people out. | Stop sending to subscribers. Keep email 7 for clickers only. Watch unsubscribes each week. |
