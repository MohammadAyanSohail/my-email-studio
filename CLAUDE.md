# Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business.

## Folders

- `brand/` - Brand voice, logo notes, tone and style guidance.
- `leads/` - Lists and notes about the people and groups we write to.
- `campaigns/` - Campaign plans and email drafts, one folder or file per campaign.
- `notes/` - Research and decisions, such as which tools and skills we chose and why.

## Rules

- Write in plain English. Keep sentences short and avoid jargon.
- Never send email to real people. Write drafts only, and let me send them myself.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
