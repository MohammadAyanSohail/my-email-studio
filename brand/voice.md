# Chaku Knives: Brand Voice Guide

Chaku Knives is a sample brand. It sells hunting and utility knives from Faisalabad, Pakistan, to outdoor enthusiasts, campers and hikers. The blades use CPM MagnaCut steel.

## Our voice in three words

1. **Fellow camper.** We write like someone who has spent real nights outdoors, not like a salesperson.
2. **Slightly rugged.** Plain, direct and calm. No fancy talk, no hype.
3. **Brotherly.** Warm and loyal, like a friend who looks out for you. Survival-minded means skill and preparedness on the trail. It never means fighting.

Our readers are serious campers and hikers. Keep the tone professional, not casual.

## Words we use

- MagnaCut (always name the steel)
- durable
- on the trail
- outside the tent
- out there
- tested by real campers and hikers (or "real hikers")
- edge, and "holds an edge"
- dependable
- built to last
- camp, trail, pack
- "brother": sparingly, at most once in an email, and only where it feels natural

## Words we avoid

- **Anything about self-defense.** Chaku Knives sells utility knives only. Never write self-defense, protection, fighting, combat, tactical or weapon-style hype. Do not let a knife be mistaken for a self-defense knife.
- **"Bro".** It sounds like gamers. "Brother" is fine, used sparingly.
- **Slang and casual hype.** Nothing like "this is so fire!" or "cool knives".
- Over-excited language: lots of exclamation marks, all capitals, "insane", "epic".

## Claims we must never make

- **Never say the blade never dulls.** Every knife needs care. Say it holds an edge, and that it needs regular maintenance.
- **Never say the steel is rust-proof or corrosion-proof.** MagnaCut is rust and corrosion resistant. The difference matters, so use "resistant".
- **Never say the blade can't break.** Very hard steel or abusive use, such as prying, hammering or twisting, can snap a blade. Say it is built for cutting, not abuse.
- **Never promise that shipping is simple or legal everywhere.** The package is declared as a weapon. Always tell buyers to check their local regulations before ordering.
- Never claim anything we cannot prove.
