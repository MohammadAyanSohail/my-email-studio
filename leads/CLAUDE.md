# Leads folder rules

These rules apply to everything in `leads/`.

- **These are made-up practice people.** Everyone in `leads.csv` is invented for testing our launch emails. None of them are real.
- **Never add real people or real email addresses.** Do not add names, emails or details of real customers, friends or contacts to this folder.
- **Every row needs a consent value.** The `consent` column must never be blank. It says how the person joined, such as `waitlist form`.
- **Never change the @example.com addresses.** Every email address must end in `@example.com`. That domain is reserved and can never receive mail, so no real person is ever emailed.
- Keep the columns exactly as they are: `first_name,last_name,email,group,joined_on,consent`.
- The three groups are `knife-curious`, `gift-buyers` and `daily-hikers`.
